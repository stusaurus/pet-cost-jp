# 主画像の制作記録

画像生成スキル `imagegen` の組み込み画像生成を使用。CLI・追加APIキーは使用していない。

採用画像：`assets/pet-home-morning.webp`（1536×1024、247,258 bytes）。生成されたPNGを、構図・画素内容を創作編集せずWebPへ配信用に変換した。既存主画像は上書きしていない。

用途：トップの暮らしの空気とOG画像。価格・仕様・互換性・商品の推奨を示す資料ではない。具体的な商品画像や数量の図は生成していない。

## 最終プロンプト

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: original editorial hero illustration for a Japanese pet household comparison website, not a UI mockup.
Primary request: the quiet joy of living with a dog and cat in a clean, naturally lit home, making everyday pet care feel warm and reassuring.
Scene/backdrop: a tidy pale oak living room corner, linen curtain and a broad softly sunlit window, an uncluttered warm ivory wall, a folded clean pet pad near a neutral litter tray, a small tidy basket for unbranded consumables.
Subject: one natural-proportioned small tan-and-white dog resting comfortably and one gray-and-white cat sitting nearby, both relaxed, curious and alive, not posed as mascots; all paws and tails anatomically natural.
Style/medium: sophisticated lifestyle-magazine gouache and colored-pencil illustration, soft hand-drawn texture, broad matte shapes with delicate edges, gentle expressive animal faces, contemporary adult editorial illustration.
Composition/framing: wide 3:2 landscape, the two animals and everyday care corner occupy the lower middle-right, sunlight and airy negative space surround them. The website places text beside the image, so no text area is needed inside. Distinct readable silhouettes even when reduced.
Lighting/mood: calm morning light, subtle warm shadows, welcoming and uplifting rather than sentimental.
Color palette: warm off-white, oatmeal, pale oak, muted sage green, restrained terracotta and dusty blue accents. No saturated colors.
Constraints: no text, no brands, no price tags, no charts, no medicine, no plants in reach of the animals, no people, no extra animals, no watermark. Pet products are unbranded illustrative objects, not real recommendations.
Avoid: photoreal stock photo, toy-like 3D, childish cartoon, oversized eyes, clutter, store advertising, crowns, sale graphics, glitter, heavy gradients.
```
